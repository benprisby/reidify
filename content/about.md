Reidify is a gaming channel started in July 2025. I play a lot of different games.
Most of what I make comes down to setting up a ridiculous situation to see how it goes:
giant monsters against tanks, robots against meteors, and plans that fall apart about
halfway through.

Most of what gets played comes straight out of the comments, so if there's something you
want to see, say so. Recording runs a few weeks ahead of posting.

For sponsorships, brand partnerships, or press, email
[business@reidify.tv](mailto:business@reidify.tv).
